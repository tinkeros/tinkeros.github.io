WebCam


Live picture from a USB webcam, in the TinkerOS extended palette (the 16 
standard colors plus the 214 web-safe ones, see GrSetExtPalette). Take pictures 
and save them as .GR files.

#include "::/Apps/WebCam/WebCam";
WebCam; 320x240 window. WebCam(640,480); for VGA, WebCam(1280,720); for 720p 
(MJPEG). A picture bigger than the window is shown at half size and saved at 
full size. WebCam(640,480,,,UVC_MJPEG); asks for MJPEG even where YUY2 has the 
size.

Keys

Space   Take a picture (the picture stays), again: back to live.
S or Enter      Save the picture as ~/Pictures/CamNNN.GR (the first free 
number). WebCam(320,240,"C:/Pics"); saves elsewhere.
D       Dithered (ordered 4x4, the default) or each pixel's nearest color.
P       All the extended palette's colors (the default) or just the 16 standard 
ones. WebCam(320,240,,,,UVC_PAL_16); starts with 16.
Esc     Quit.

Streaming window

CamStream;      The picture at half size in a small window in the lower right 
corner that stays on top and never takes the keyboard, so you keep working in 
your own window. CamStream(640,480); for a 320x240 window, CamStream(320,240,FAL
SE,UVC_PAL_16); for 16 colors without dithering (arguments: width, height, 
dither, colors, camera, format).
CamStream16;    The same in the 16 standard colors, dithered (CamStream16(320,24
0,FALSE);: nearest of the 16, not dithered).
CamStreamColors(UVC_PAL_ALL);   Change a running stream's colors from the next 
frame: UVC_PAL_ALL or UVC_PAL_16, then dithered (TRUE, the default) or not.
CamStreamStop;  Close it (CamStream; again does too). One of WebCam and CamStrea
m at a time: they share the camera.

Other calls

CamSnap;        One 640x480 picture without a window (CamSnap(,1280,720); for 
720p): saves ~/Pictures/CamNNN.GR and returns its name.
CamRep; The camera's formats, frame sizes, frame rates and streaming bandwidths.
CamDiag;        Stream 320x240 for 3 seconds and report: frames, why any were 
dropped, the payloads and the kernel's counters. CamDiag(640,480); for another 
size, CamDiag(640,480,3,UVC_MJPEG); for MJPEG.
CamDiagAll;     Each common size and frame rate for 2 seconds, one line each: 
frames whole or short (and by how many lines), MJPEG frames decoded.
CamCap; Record every packet for 4 seconds into ~/TOSXfr/cap.bin (length and 
payload header each) for tools/uvccap.py on another machine.

Pictures open in GrPaint and anything else that reads .GR files (GRRead).

Cameras

USB Video Class (UVC) cameras that send uncompressed YUY2 or MJPEG, on EHCI 
(high speed) or xHCI. YUY2 is used when the camera has the size, MJPEG otherwise 
(often the only way to get 720p). That includes the built-in cameras of the 
ThinkPad T420 (Chicony 04f2:b221), T430 (04f2:b2db, 04f2:b2ea) and T440p (Bison 
5986:0268), and most external USB 2.0 webcams plugged into an EHCI port. UsbRep; 
lists a camera as video.

Not supported: H.264-only cameras. Full-speed (USB 1.1) cameras on EHCI go 
through the hub's split transactions (siTDs): written to the EHCI spec, not 
tested on real hardware yet.

How it works

The kernel enumerates the camera (USB_KIND_VIDEO) and streams its isochronous 
endpoint (EHCI iTDs or siTDs, xHCI Isoch TRBs: UsbVenIsoStart, UsbVenIsoRead). 
Uvc.HC does the rest: descriptors, probe/commit, payload headers, frame assembly 
and the conversion to the palette. Jpeg.HC decodes MJPEG frames, and any 
baseline .jpg: JpgFileDecode("photo.jpg",&w,&h) gives RGB.
