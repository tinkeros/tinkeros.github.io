Tetris

                          Classic Falling Block Puzzle

Rotate and place falling pieces to complete solid horizontal lines.
Completed lines vanish and everything above drops down.  Do not let the stack
reach the top!

Controls
  Left / Right   - Move piece
  Up             - Rotate piece
  Down           - Soft drop (fall faster)
  Space          - Hard drop (instantly place)
  P              - Pause / unpause
  Enter          - Restart
  ESC            - Quit

Scoring
  Clearing 1 line  = 100 pts x Level.
  Clearing 4 lines = 800 pts x Level.

  Level increases every 10 lines; speed increases with each level.

Pieces
  I (cyan), O (yellow), T (purple), S (green), Z (red), J (blue), L (orange)

Tips
  Keep the stack flat and low.
  Try to leave room for the I-piece to score 4 lines at a time.
  Use the next-piece preview to plan rotations in advance.
  Plant crosses standing up to destroy surrounding rows and columns.
