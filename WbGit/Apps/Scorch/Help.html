Scorch

                              Scorched Earth Clone

Four tanks battle across a procedurally generated landscape.  Adjust barrel 
angle
and fire power, then launch your shell and watch the trajectory arc.  First to
win three rounds wins the match!

Controls
  Left / Right   - Adjust barrel angle
  Up / Down      - Increase / decrease fire power
  Space / Enter  - Fire!
  R              - New game
  ESC            - Quit

Players
  Up to 4 players take turns.  Each player sets angle and power then fires.
  Terrain is destroyed by shell impacts -- the landscape changes as the battle
  progresses.

Objective
  Game ends after one player wins 3 rounds.
  Direct hits score the most damage; near misses still deal splash damage.
  Score by hitting and killings tanks.
  Terry is the better AI player and worth more points.

Tips
  Account for wind (shown in HUD) when aiming.
  High power and steep angles can drop shells behind hills.
  Destroy terrain beneath enemies to hurt them also.
  Terry regenerates health and aims better as number of players decreases.
  You lose points for dying, damaging yourself, or not winning a round.
