You have traveled back in time to save Terry from the aliens and glowies.

Terry is counting on you to help stay fed with BK burgers and Diet Shasta.

Distracted by his lost bird he risks not seeing UFOs and getting abducted!


Controls

  Left / Right   - Thrust in that direction
  Up / Down      - Adjust altitude
  Space          - Fire (also continues on the WAVE CLEAR screen)
  B              - BK boost (destroys all on-screen UFOs)
  S              - Shasta boost (30s faster ship AND Terry)
  H              - Hyper-jump to Terry's location (once per wave)
  R              - Restart (on the GAME OVER screen)
  ESC            - Quit

Mission Briefing:

  - UFOs swoop down to carry glowies to safety in space.

  - Rescued glowies turn UFOs into a kamikaze UFOs headed for your ship!

  - Terry will handle the glowies on the ground with his car!

  - Terry also picks up items on the ground for your shared inventory.

  - A wave ends when every UFO and glowie is gone (even without the bird!)

  - If a UFO grabs Terry, an alert flashes, rescue him or it is GAME OVER!

  - Once per wave you can hyper-jump to him if he is in trouble.

  - Use BK, Shasta, and recover the bird for the King Terry Bonus!

  - Green check marks and a crown track your bonus progress for the wave.

  - When King Terry is achieved Terry can help kill landed UFOs with his car!

Tips:

  - Radar at the top tracks the bird, bonus items, Glowies, Terry and UFOs.

  - Terrified by aliens, glowies, and UFOs Terry's bird escapes early each wave.

  - Terry's bird appears PURPLE on your radar at the top of the sceen.

  - Terry's bird will try to fly away from you, use a Shasta boost to help.

  - Terry's bird may escape if Terry gets abducted.

  - Hyper-jump followed by BK boost is a powerful combo to protect Terry.

  - Wait to kill all the UFOs until you have the bird if going for the bonus.
